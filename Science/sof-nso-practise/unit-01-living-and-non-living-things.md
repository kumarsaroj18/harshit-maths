# 🌱 Unit 1 — Living and Non-Living Things

**SOF NSO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 1 of 5**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit covers the basic differences between living and non-living things. Help your child spot examples around the house!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know what **living things** are and their characteristics
- Know what **non-living things** are
- Understand that living things **need food, water, and air**
- Learn that living things **grow, move, and reproduce**
- Identify examples of **living and non-living things** around us

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. What are Living Things?**

Living things are things that are **alive**. They can:
- **Breathe** (need air)
- **Eat** (need food)
- **Drink** (need water)
- **Grow** (become bigger)
- **Move** (on their own)
- **Reproduce** (have babies)
- **Feel** (respond to things around them)

**Examples:** Humans, animals, birds, insects, fish, plants, trees

---

**2. What are Non-Living Things?**

Non-living things are things that are **not alive**. They:
- Do NOT breathe
- Do NOT eat or drink
- Do NOT grow on their own
- Do NOT have babies
- Cannot move on their own

**Examples:** Chair, table, stone, water, toys, books, car, bicycle

---

**3. Special Cases**

- **Plants** are living things even though they don't walk around!
- **Dead things** (like dry leaves, wooden furniture) were once living but are not alive now
- **Water, air** are non-living but living things need them to survive

---

**4. Easy Way to Remember**

Ask: "Can it breathe? Can it grow on its own? Can it have babies?"
- If YES to all → **Living**
- If NO to all → **Non-Living**
