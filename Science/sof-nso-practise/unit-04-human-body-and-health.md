# 🧍 Unit 4 — Human Body and Health

**SOF NSO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 4 of 5**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit covers body parts, sense organs, food groups, and healthy habits. Make it fun by pointing to body parts and discussing healthy choices!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know the **external body parts** and their functions
- Learn about the **five sense organs** and what they do
- Understand **food groups** and healthy eating
- Know **good habits** for staying healthy
- Learn about **safety rules** and hygiene

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. External Body Parts**

| Part | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Head** | Contains brain, eyes, ears, nose, mouth |
| **Eyes** | Help us see |
| **Ears** | Help us hear |
| **Nose** | Helps us smell and breathe |
| **Mouth** | Helps us eat, drink, and talk |
| **Hands** | Help us hold, write, and do work |
| **Legs** | Help us walk, run, and jump |
| **Heart** | Pumps blood to all body parts |
| **Lungs** | Help us breathe |
| **Stomach** | Digests the food we eat |

---

**2. The Five Sense Organs**

| Sense Organ | Sense | What We Do |
|-------------|-------|------------|
| **Eyes** | Sight | See colors, shapes, people |
| **Ears** | Hearing | Hear sounds, music, voices |
| **Nose** | Smell | Smell flowers, food, perfume |
| **Tongue** | Taste | Taste sweet, sour, salty, bitter |
| **Skin** | Touch | Feel hot, cold, soft, rough |

---

**3. Types of Food**

- **Energy-giving foods** — Rice, bread, chapati, potatoes (give us energy to play and work)
- **Body-building foods** — Milk, eggs, fish, pulses (help us grow strong)
- **Protective foods** — Fruits and vegetables (keep us healthy, fight diseases)

---

**4. Good Habits for Health**

✅ Brush teeth twice a day
✅ Take bath daily
✅ Wash hands before eating
✅ Eat healthy food
✅ Drink plenty of water
✅ Exercise and play
✅ Sleep on time (8-10 hours)
✅ Keep nails short and clean

---

**5. Safety Rules**

⚠️ Do not play on the road
⚠️ Cross the road at zebra crossing
⚠️ Do not touch sharp objects
⚠️ Do not talk to strangers
⚠️ Do not play with fire or matches
⚠️ Always wear a helmet while cycling
