# 🦁 Unit 3 — Animals

**SOF NSO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 3 of 5**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit covers types of animals, their homes, food habits, and how they move. Visit a zoo or watch animal videos together!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know the difference between **domestic and wild animals**
- Learn about **animal homes** (shelter)
- Understand what animals eat — **herbivores, carnivores, omnivores**
- Know how different animals **move** (walk, fly, swim, crawl)
- Learn about **animal babies** and their names

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Types of Animals**

**Domestic Animals** — Animals that live with humans
- Dog, Cat, Cow, Horse, Goat, Hen, Sheep
- They give us milk, eggs, wool, or help us

**Wild Animals** — Animals that live in forests/jungles
- Lion, Tiger, Elephant, Bear, Wolf, Deer
- They find their own food and shelter

**Pet Animals** — Animals we keep at home for company
- Dog, Cat, Fish, Parrot, Rabbit

---

**2. What Animals Eat**

| Type | What They Eat | Examples |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Herbivores** | Only plants | Cow, Goat, Rabbit, Deer, Elephant |
| **Carnivores** | Only other animals | Lion, Tiger, Wolf, Eagle |
| **Omnivores** | Both plants and animals | Dog, Bear, Crow, Humans |

---

**4. How Animals Move**

- **Walk/Run** — Dog, Cat, Cow, Lion

- **Hop/Jump** — Frog, Rabbit, Kangaroo

## ✅ Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|:---:|---|
| 1 | (B) Cow | A cow lives with humans and gives us milk — it is a domestic animal |
| 2 | (D) Tiger | A tiger lives in the jungle and hunts for food — it is a wild animal |
| 3 | (C) Kennel | A dog's house is called a kennel |
| 4 | (B) Cow | Cows give us milk that we drink |
| 5 | (D) Nest | Birds build nests in trees using twigs, leaves, and grass |
| 6 | (D) Parrot | A parrot is a bird with wings — it can fly |
| 7 | (A) Puppy | A baby dog is called a puppy |
| 8 | (D) Fish | Fish live in water and swim using their fins |
| 9 | (A) Hen | Hens lay eggs that we eat |
| 10 | (C) Crawling | Snakes have no legs — they crawl on the ground |
| 11 | (C) Herbivores | Herbivores eat only plants (grass, leaves, fruits) |
| 12 | (C) Carnivores | Carnivores hunt and eat other animals |
| 13 | (A) Bear | Bears eat both plants (berries) and animals (fish) — they are omnivores |
| 14 | (A) Den | A lion's home is called a den |
| 15 | (B) Frog | Frogs have strong back legs and hop to move |
| 16 | (A) Kitten | A baby cat is called a kitten |
| 17 | (A) Hive | Bees live together in a hive and make honey there |
| 18 | (B) Sheep | Sheep have soft, fuzzy hair that we use to make wool |
| 19 | (C) Burrow | Rabbits dig holes called burrows to live in |
| 20 | (D) Goldfish | Goldfish are kept in tanks at home as pets |
| 21 | (B) Stable | A horse's home is called a stable |
| 22 | (C) Tortoise | A tortoise carries a hard shell on its back for protection |
| 23 | (A) Calf | A baby cow is called a calf |
| 24 | (B) Web | Spiders spin webs to trap insects for food |
| 25 | (A) Frog | Frogs can live on land and in water — they are amphibians |
| 26 | (D) No, bats can fly but they are not birds | Bats can fly but they are mammals, not birds. Insects like butterflies can also fly |
| 27 | (B) Its wings are too small for flying but help in swimming | Penguins have small, flipper-like wings that help them swim, not fly |
| 28 | (B) Has a tail but no legs | Tadpoles have tails and no legs. As they grow, they develop legs and lose their tail |
| 29 | (D) Can travel long distances in the desert without much water | Camels can store water in their body and walk long distances in hot deserts carrying heavy loads |
| 30 | (C) Buffalo | A buffalo also gives milk, has horns, and makes a sound similar to "moo" |
