## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Air**

**What is air?**
- Air is all around us, but we cannot see it
- Air has no color, no smell, no taste
- Air fills up space and has weight
- Moving air is called **wind**

**Why do we need air?**
- All living things need air to **breathe**
- Plants need air (carbon dioxide) to make food
- Fire needs air to burn
- Air helps birds fly and kites soar

---

**2. Water**

**Sources of Water:**
- **Rain** — falls from clouds
- **Rivers** — flowing water
- **Lakes and Ponds** — still water
- **Wells and Tube wells** — underground water
- **Sea/Ocean** — salty water (not for drinking)

**Uses of Water:**
- Drinking, cooking, bathing
- Washing clothes and dishes
- Watering plants
- For animals to drink
- Generating electricity

**States of Water:**
- **Solid** — Ice (when water is frozen)
- **Liquid** — Water (normal form)
- **Gas** — Steam/Water vapor (when water is heated)

---

**3. Weather**

Weather tells us if the day is:
- ☀️ **Sunny** — bright, hot
- 🌧️ **Rainy** — wet, cloudy
- ☁️ **Cloudy** — sky covered with clouds
- 💨 **Windy** — lots of moving air
- ❄️ **Cold/Snowy** — very cold, may have snow

---

**4. Seasons**

| Season | Months (India) | Weather |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| **Summer** | March – June | Very hot, drink lots of water |
| **Rainy (Monsoon)** | July – September | Rain, umbrellas needed |
| **Autumn** | October – November | Cool, leaves fall |
| **Winter** | December – February | Cold, wear warm clothes |
| **Spring** | February – March | Flowers bloom, pleasant |

---

**5. Water Cycle (Simple)**

1. Sun heats water in rivers, lakes, oceans
2. Water turns into **water vapor** (evaporation)
3. Vapor goes up and forms **clouds**
4. Clouds become heavy and water falls as **rain**
5. Rain water goes back to rivers and oceans

This goes on again and again — it's called the **water cycle**!
