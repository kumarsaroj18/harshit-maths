## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know the **parts of a plant** (root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit, seed)
- Understand what **plants need** to grow (sunlight, water, air, soil)
- Learn about **different types of plants** (trees, shrubs, herbs, climbers, creepers)
- Know the **uses of plants** (food, medicine, wood, oxygen)
- Understand that plants make their **own food** using sunlight

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Parts of a Plant**

- **Root** — grows underground; absorbs water and minerals; holds the plant in soil
- **Stem** — carries water from roots to leaves; holds the plant upright
- **Leaf** — makes food for the plant using sunlight (green color from chlorophyll)
- **Flower** — helps the plant make seeds; often colorful and fragrant
- **Fruit** — grows from the flower; contains seeds inside
- **Seed** — can grow into a new plant

---

**2. What Plants Need to Grow**

- **Sunlight** — for making food
- **Water** — for drinking and making food
- **Air** — plants breathe too!
- **Soil** — gives nutrients and holds the plant

---

**3. Types of Plants**

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Trees** | Big, tall, hard wooden stem (trunk) | Mango, Neem, Banyan |
| **Shrubs** | Medium-sized, many stems from the ground | Rose, Hibiscus |
| **Herbs** | Small plants with soft stems | Mint, Tulsi, Coriander |
| **Climbers** | Climb up walls or other plants | Money plant, Pea plant |
| **Creepers** | Spread along the ground | Pumpkin, Watermelon |

---

**4. Uses of Plants**

- **Food** — fruits, vegetables, grains
- **Medicine** — Tulsi, Neem, Aloe vera
- **Wood** — furniture, paper, houses
- **Oxygen** — plants give us fresh air to breathe
- **Shade & Beauty** — gardens, parks

## ✅ Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|:---:|---|
| 1 | (D) Root | Roots grow underground and absorb water from the soil |
| 2 | (B) Leaf | Leaves make food for the plant using sunlight |
| 3 | (A) Sunlight | Plants use sunlight to make their own food |
| 4 | (C) Tree | A mango tree is big and tall with a hard wooden trunk — it is a tree |
| 5 | (D) Seeds | Fruits contain seeds inside that can grow into new plants |
| 6 | (B) Stem | The stem holds the plant upright and carries water to the leaves |
| 7 | (B) Shrub | Rose is a medium-sized plant with many stems — it is a shrub |
| 8 | (A) Green | Leaves are green because they contain chlorophyll |
| 9 | (A) Mint | Mint is a small plant with a soft stem — it is a herb |
| 10 | (C) Oxygen | Plants release oxygen that we breathe in to stay alive |
| 11 | (A) Money plant | Money plant is a climber — it climbs up walls and supports |
| 12 | (D) Creeper | Plants that spread along the ground are called creepers |
| 13 | (B) Water and minerals | Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil for the plant |
| 14 | (A) Toys | Plants need sunlight, water, and air — not toys! |
| 15 | (C) New plant | When a seed gets water, air, and warmth, it grows into a new plant |
| 16 | (D) Flower | A flower turns into a fruit after pollination |
| 17 | (D) Trees | Trees have hard wooden stems (trunks) that give us wood |
| 18 | (B) Root | Carrot is a root vegetable that grows underground |
| 19 | (A) Spinach | Spinach (palak) is a leafy vegetable — we eat its leaves |
| 20 | (C) Medicine | Tulsi leaves are used as medicine for cough and cold |
| 21 | (A) Banyan tree | Banyan tree has a thick, hard wooden trunk |
| 22 | (D) Flower | Cauliflower is the flower part of the plant that we eat |
| 23 | (B) Stem | Potato is an underground stem, not a root |
| 24 | (C) Mango tree | Mango tree is big and gives tasty mangoes as well as cool shade |
| 25 | (C) Herbs | Herbs are small plants with soft, green stems that live for a short time |
| 26 | (D) It did not get sunlight | Plants need sunlight to make food and stay healthy. Without sunlight, they become pale and weak |
| 27 | (B) Underground stem | Ginger is an underground stem (like potato), not a root |
| 28 | (A) Thick and fleshy | Cactus has a thick, fleshy stem to store water for dry times in the desert |
| 29 | (B) The plant will not be able to make food | Leaves make food for the plant. Without leaves, the plant cannot make food and will die |
| 30 | (C) Can move towards light | Plants grow towards sunlight because they need it to make food. This movement is called phototropism |
