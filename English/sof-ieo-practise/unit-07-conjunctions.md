## 💡 Learn It First

**What is a Conjunction?**
A conjunction is a **joining word**. It connects two words, phrases, or sentences together.

```
I like apples AND oranges. (joins two words)
She is tired BUT happy. (joins two ideas)
Do you want milk OR juice? (gives a choice)
```

**Common Conjunctions:**

| Conjunction | What it does | Example |
|-------------|--------------|---------|
| **and** | adds/joins similar things | I have a cat **and** a dog. |
| **but** | shows difference/contrast | She is small **but** strong. |
| **or** | gives a choice | Is it a boy **or** a girl? |
| **because** | gives a reason | I am happy **because** I won! |
| **so** | shows result | I was hungry, **so** I ate. |

**And vs But vs Or:**
- **AND** = Both things are true or wanted → I like pizza **and** pasta.
- **BUT** = One thing is different from expected → He is young **but** wise.
- **OR** = Only one of the things → Tea **or** coffee?

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Conjunction
1. **Ask:** Am I adding things? → **and**
2. **Ask:** Am I showing something different/opposite? → **but**
3. **Ask:** Am I giving choices? → **or**
4. **Ask:** Am I giving a reason? → **because**
5. **Ask:** Am I showing what happened next? → **so**
