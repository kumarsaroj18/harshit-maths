## 💡 Learn It First

**What is Reading Comprehension?**
It means reading a passage and understanding what it says. You will answer questions based on what is written.

**Tips for Reading Comprehension:**
1. **Read the passage carefully** — don't rush!
2. **Read the questions** — understand what is being asked
3. **Go back to the passage** — find the answer in the text
4. **Don't guess** — the answer is always in the passage!

**Types of Questions:**
- **Who** did something?
- **What** happened?
- **Where** did it happen?
- **When** did it happen?
- **Why** did it happen?
