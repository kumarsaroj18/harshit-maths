# ✏️ Unit 8 — Spellings

**SOF IEO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 8 of 14**

> **Parent's Note:** Spellings need regular practice! Try the "Look-Cover-Write-Check" method: Look at the word, cover it, write it from memory, then check. Make spelling practice fun with word games and flashcards!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Spelling common **everyday words** correctly
- Words related to **animals**, **body parts**, **clothes**, **food**, **emotions**
- Spotting the **correct spelling** among wrong options
- Finding the **wrongly spelled word** in a group

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**Why Spellings Matter:**
In the IEO exam, you might be asked to:
1. Choose the correctly spelled word
2. Find the wrongly spelled word
3. Fill in the missing letters

**Common Spelling Rules:**

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| "i" before "e" except after "c" | bel**ie**ve, rec**ei**ve |
| Silent letters | **k**now, **w**rite, lis**t**en |
| Double letters | happ**y** → happ**i**ness, runn**ing** |

**Words You Must Know (Class 2 Level):**

**Animals:** elephant, giraffe, monkey, rabbit, butterfly, parrot, tortoise

**Body Parts:** shoulder, stomach, finger, tongue, knee, elbow, ankle

**Clothes:** shirt, trousers, sweater, jacket, socks, shoes, dress

**Food:** breakfast, biscuit, chocolate, vegetables, sandwich, juice

**Emotions:** happy, angry, scared, excited, surprised, worried

**School:** pencil, eraser, scissors, teacher, library, school
