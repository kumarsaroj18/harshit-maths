## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- What a **noun** is — a word that names a person, place, animal, or thing
- **Common nouns** — general names (boy, city, dog)
- **Proper nouns** — special names that start with a capital letter (Rahul, Mumbai, Rex)
- Spotting nouns in sentences

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**What is a Noun?**
A noun is a **naming word**. It tells us the name of a person, place, animal, or thing.

| Type | What it names | Examples |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Person | people | mother, teacher, Amit, doctor |
| Place | locations | school, park, Delhi, kitchen |
| Animal | creatures | dog, cat, lion, Polly (pet's name) |
| Thing | objects/ideas | book, ball, happiness, table |

**Common Noun vs Proper Noun:**
- **Common noun** = general name → boy, city, river (no capital letter)
- **Proper noun** = special/specific name → Rohan, Jaipur, Ganga (always starts with a CAPITAL letter)

```
Common: I have a dog.
Proper: My dog's name is Bruno.
```

### 🧠 How to Find a Noun — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Ask:** Can I see it, touch it, or name it?
2. **Check:** Is it a person, place, animal, or thing?
3. **Look:** Does it start with a capital letter? (If yes, it might be a proper noun!)
