## 💡 Learn It First

**What is a Collocation?**
A collocation is when certain words naturally go together. Native speakers just "know" these combinations.

```
✔️ make a mistake (correct)
❌ do a mistake (wrong — sounds odd!)

✔️ do homework (correct)
❌ make homework (wrong)
```

**Common Collocations:**

| With MAKE | With DO | With HAVE | With TAKE |
|-----------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| make the bed | do homework | have breakfast | take a bath |
| make a cake | do the dishes | have fun | take a photo |
| make friends | do your best | have a dream | take a rest |
| make a wish | do exercise | have a cold | take a walk |
| make noise | do a favour | have a party | take care |

**Other Common Collocations:**

| First Word | Goes With |
|------------|-----------|
| brush | teeth, hair |
| catch | a ball, a cold, a bus |
| tell | a story, a lie, the truth |
| fast | food, asleep |
| heavy | rain, traffic |
| strong | wind, coffee |

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Collocation
1. **Think:** Have I heard these words together before?
2. **Say it:** Does it sound natural?
3. **Remember:** Some combinations are fixed — learn them by heart!
