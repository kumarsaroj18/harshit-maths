## 💡 Learn It First

**What is the Achievers Section?**
This is the most challenging part of the IEO exam. These questions:
- Are worth **2 marks each** (instead of 1 mark)
- Test **multiple concepts** in one question
- Need **careful thinking** — not just memory!

**Types of Achievers Questions:**
1. Grammar combinations (articles + prepositions + tenses together)
2. Error finding in sentences
3. Sentence rearrangement
4. Paragraph completion
5. Logic-based language questions

### 🧠 Tips for Achievers Section
1. **Read the question twice** — understand what is being asked
2. **Eliminate wrong options** — cross out obviously wrong answers
3. **Check your answer** — read the sentence with your answer to see if it sounds right
4. **Don't rush** — these are worth more marks!

## Section B — Error Finding and Correction (Q11–Q20) 🏅

**Q11.** Find the error and choose the correction:
"Their are three apples on the table."
(A) Their → There
(B) apples → apple
(C) are → is
(D) on → in

**Q12.** Which word is WRONGLY used?
"She is the more beautiful girl in the class."
(A) She
(B) girl
(C) more
(D) class

**Q13.** Find the error: "I have went to the market yesterday."
(A) have went → went
(B) I → Me
(C) to → at
(D) yesterday → today

**Q14.** Choose the sentence with correct word order:
(A) The cat quickly ran.
(B) The cat ran quickly.
(C) Quickly the cat ran.
(D) Ran the cat quickly.

**Q15.** Find the ERROR: "Him and me went to the park."
(A) Him → He
(B) me → I
(C) Both A and B
(D) went → go

**Q16.** Which underlined word is INCORRECT?
"My **mother's** friend **gived** me a **beautiful** present."
(A) beautiful
(B) gived
(C) mother's
(D) present

**Q17.** Find the sentence that is COMPLETELY correct:
(A) They doesn't play cricket.
(B) i likes to plays football.

(D) He don't like ice cream.

**Q18.** What is wrong? "An university is near my house."

**Q19.** Correct the sentence: "She have two brother and one sister."

**Q20.** Find the error: "Their going to the zoo tomorrow."

## Section C — Complex Comprehension and Logic (Q21–Q30) ⭐

**Read the passage and answer Q21-Q25:**

> **The Clever Fox**
> 
> One day, a thirsty crow found a pot with a little water at the bottom. The crow's beak could not reach the water. The crow thought and thought. Then it had an idea!
> 
> The crow picked up small stones and dropped them into the pot one by one. Slowly, the water rose higher and higher. Finally, the crow could drink the water. The crow flew away happily.
> 
> A fox was watching from behind a tree. "What a clever bird!" said the fox. "It used its brain instead of giving up."

**Q21.** What was the crow's problem?
(A) The water was too cold
(B) The crow's beak couldn't reach the water
(C) The pot was too heavy
(D) There was no water

**Q22.** How did the crow solve the problem?
(A) It dropped stones to raise the water level
(B) It waited for rain
(C) It broke the pot
(D) It called for help

**Q23.** What does "instead of giving up" mean?
(A) The crow didn't stop trying
(B) The crow ran away
(C) The crow was angry
(D) The crow was scared

**Q24.** The title says "The Clever Fox" but the story is about a clever ___?
(A) Stone
(B) Pot
(C) Fox
(D) Crow

**Q25.** What lesson does this story teach?
(A) Use your brain to solve problems
(B) Crows are better than foxes
(C) Water is important
(D) Foxes are sneaky

## ✅ Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|:---:|---|
| 1 | (C) An, a, in | An elephant (vowel sound), a big animal (consonant), in the jungle (place) |
| 2 | (B) He is reading a story. | Correct subject-verb agreement and structure |
| 3 | (B) went, bought | Yesterday = past tense; go→went, buy→bought |
| 4 | (D) Where is the book? | Capital W, question mark at end |
| 5 | (C) He, me | Subject pronoun "He" does the action; object pronoun "me" receives |
| 6 | (B) The **butterfly** sat on a **beautiful** flower. | Both words spelled correctly |
| 7 | (D) go, We | My sister and I = we → go (not goes); We (same subject) |
| 8 | (A) A cat is sitting on the table. | A (consonant), on (not in for sitting), cat is (singular) |
| 9 | (D) but, so | Contrast (wanted but raining), Result (so stayed home) |
| 10 | (A) Riya, brother, Delhi, Monday — 4 nouns | Riya (person), brother (person), Delhi (place), Monday (proper noun-day) |
| 11 | (A) Their → There | "There are" for existence; "Their" shows possession |
| 12 | (C) more | Should be "most beautiful" (superlative), not "more beautiful" |
| 13 | (A) have went → went | Past tense doesn't use "have went"; just "went" |
| 14 | (B) The cat ran quickly. | Subject + Verb + Adverb (natural order) |
| 15 | (C) Both A and B | Should be "He and I went" (subject pronouns) |
| 16 | (B) gived | Should be "gave" (give→gave, not gived) |
| 17 | (C) She doesn't like vegetables. | Correct use of doesn't with she |
| 18 | (B) An → A | University sounds like "you-niversity" (consonant sound) |
| 19 | (A) She has two brothers and one sister. | has (not have), brothers (plural) |
| 20 | (D) Their → They're | They're = They are; Their = possession |
| 21 | (B) The crow's beak couldn't reach the water | "The crow's beak could not reach the water" |
| 22 | (A) It dropped stones to raise the water level | "picked up small stones and dropped them" |
| 23 | (A) The crow didn't stop trying | The crow kept trying instead of quitting |
| 24 | (D) Crow | The story is about the crow; fox just watched |
| 25 | (A) Use your brain to solve problems | "used its brain instead of giving up" |
| 26 | (D) Hello! I'm Sameer. What's your name? Welcome to our school! | Polite introduction and welcome |
| 27 | (B) May I sit with you, please? | Polite permission request |
| 28 | (D) You're welcome! See you tomorrow! | Polite response to thanks |
| 29 | (C) She always helps the poor. | Correct sentence order |
| 30 | (C) write | Write name, write work, write and colour — all use "write" |
