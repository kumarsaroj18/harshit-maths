## 💡 Learn It First

**What is an Adjective?**
An adjective is a **describing word**. It tells us more about a noun (person, place, animal, or thing).

```
The big dog barks. (big describes the dog)
The sky is blue. (blue describes the sky)
I have three books. (three tells how many)
```

**Types of Adjectives:**

| Type | What it tells | Examples |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Colour | What colour? | red, blue, green, yellow, pink |
| Size | How big/small? | big, small, tall, short, long |
| Shape | What shape? | round, square, flat, straight |
| Number | How many? | one, two, three, many, few |
| Feeling | How does it feel? | happy, sad, angry, scared, excited |
| Quality | What kind? | good, bad, beautiful, ugly, clean, dirty |

**Where do adjectives go?**
1. **Before the noun:** The **tall** boy is running.
2. **After is/am/are:** The boy is **tall**.

### 🧠 How to Find an Adjective
1. **Find the noun** first
2. **Ask:** What kind? What colour? How many? How big?
3. **The answer** is usually the adjective!
