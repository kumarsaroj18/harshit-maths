> **Parent's Note:** Punctuation marks are like traffic signals for reading! They tell us when to stop (full stop), pause (comma), or get excited (exclamation mark). Practice by reading aloud together and noticing where the voice pauses or rises.

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- What **punctuation marks** are and why we use them
- **Full stop (.)** — at the end of a sentence
- **Question mark (?)** — at the end of a question
- **Exclamation mark (!)** — to show strong feeling
- **Comma (,)** — to separate items or take a short pause
- **Capital letters** — at the beginning of sentences and for names

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**What is Punctuation?**
Punctuation marks are symbols that help us read and understand sentences better. They show where to stop, pause, or ask.

**Common Punctuation Marks:**

| Mark | Name | When to Use | Example |
|------|------|-------------|---------|
| . | Full stop / Period | At the end of a statement | I like pizza**.** |
| ? | Question mark | At the end of a question | What is your name**?** |
| ! | Exclamation mark | To show excitement, surprise, or command | Wow! / Stop**!** |
| , | Comma | To separate items in a list or for a pause | I have pens**,** pencils**,** and erasers**.** |
| ' | Apostrophe | For contractions or possession | don**'**t, Riya**'**s book |

**Capital Letters (A, B, C... not a, b, c...):**
Use capitals for:
1. The first word of every sentence
2. The word "I"
3. Names of people: **R**iya, **A**mit
4. Names of places: **D**elhi, **I**ndia
5. Days and months: **M**onday, **J**anuary

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Punctuation
1. **Statement** (telling something) → Full stop (.)
2. **Question** (asking something) → Question mark (?)
3. **Strong feeling/Command** → Exclamation mark (!)
4. **List of things** → Commas (,) between items
