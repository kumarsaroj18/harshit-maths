## 💡 Learn It First

**What are Articles?**
Articles are small words that come before nouns. There are only THREE articles in English:
- **a** (for general things, consonant sounds)
- **an** (for general things, vowel sounds)
- **the** (for specific things)

**A vs An — The Sound Rule:**

| Use | When the next word starts with | Examples |
|-----|-------------------------------|----------|
| **a** | consonant sound (b, c, d, f, g, h, j, k, l, m, n, p, q, r, s, t, v, w, x, y, z) | a ball, a cat, a dog |
| **an** | vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u) | an apple, an egg, an ice cream |

**Special Cases:**
- **an** hour (h is silent, sounds like "our")
- **a** university (u sounds like "you" — consonant sound)
- **an** umbrella (u sounds like "uh" — vowel sound)

**When to use "the":**
- When there is **only one** of something: the sun, the moon
- When we are talking about **something specific**: the book on the table
- When we **already mentioned** it: I saw a cat. **The** cat was white.

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Article
1. **Listen** to the SOUND of the next word (not just the letter)
2. **Vowel sound** → an
3. **Consonant sound** → a
4. **Specific/Only one** → the
