- **Time prepositions**: at, in, on
- **Direction prepositions**: to, from, into, out of

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**What is a Preposition?**
A preposition is a word that tells us **where** something is, **when** something happens, or **which direction** something goes.

```
The cat is ON the table. (where?)
School starts AT 8 o'clock. (when?)
She walked TO the park. (direction?)
```

**Place Prepositions (Where?):**

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| in | inside something | The ball is **in** the box. |
| on | touching the top of | The book is **on** the table. |
| under | below something | The dog is **under** the bed. |
| behind | at the back of | He hid **behind** the door. |
| in front of | before something | She stood **in front of** the class. |
| beside / next to | at the side of | Sit **beside** me. |
| between | in the middle of two things | The park is **between** two buildings. |
| near | close to | My house is **near** the school. |

**Time Prepositions (When?):**

| Preposition | Use with | Example |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| at | time | **at** 5 o'clock, **at** noon |
| in | month, year, part of day | **in** June, **in** 2024, **in** the morning |
| on | day, date | **on** Monday, **on** 15th August |

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Preposition
1. **Ask:** Where is it? When is it? Which direction?
2. **Picture it** in your mind
3. **Choose** the preposition that matches the picture
