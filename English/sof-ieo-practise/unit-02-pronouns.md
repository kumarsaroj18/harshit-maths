## 💡 Learn It First

**What is a Pronoun?**
A pronoun is a word that **takes the place of a noun** so we don't have to repeat the same name again and again.

```
❌ Riya likes Riya's bag. Riya takes Riya's bag to school.
✔️ Riya likes her bag. She takes it to school.
```

**Personal Pronouns Chart:**

| Person | Subject (does the action) | Object (receives the action) |
|--------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| Talking about myself | I | me |
| Talking to someone | you | you |
| Talking about a boy/man | he | him |
| Talking about a girl/woman | she | her |
| Talking about a thing/animal | it | it |
| Talking about ourselves (group) | we | us |
| Talking about others (group) | they | them |

**Examples:**
- **I** am happy. (I = myself)
- **He** is my brother. (He = a boy)
- **She** likes to dance. (She = a girl)
- **It** is raining. (It = the weather/thing)
- **We** are playing. (We = my group)
- **They** are coming. (They = other people)

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Pronoun
1. **Who/What** is the sentence talking about?
2. **Is it** one person or many people?
3. **Is the person** doing the action or receiving the action?
