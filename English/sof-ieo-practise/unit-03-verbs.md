---

## 💡 Learn It First

**What is a Verb?**
A verb is a **doing word** or **action word**. It tells us what someone or something is doing.

```
The bird flies. (flies = action)
I eat breakfast. (eat = action)
She is happy. (is = state of being)
```

**Two Types of Verbs:**

| Type | What it does | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Action verb | Shows an action | run, jump, eat, drink, read, write, sleep, play |
| Helping verb | Helps the main verb | is, am, are, was, were, has, have |

**Using is, am, are:**
| Subject | Helping Verb | Example |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| I | am | I **am** a student. |
| He/She/It | is | She **is** happy. |
| You/We/They | are | They **are** playing. |

### 🧠 How to Find a Verb — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Ask:** What is the person or thing doing?
2. **Check:** Can you act it out? (run, jump, eat, sleep)
3. **Look:** Is there a "be" verb? (is, am, are)
