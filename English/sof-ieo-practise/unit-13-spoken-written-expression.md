## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- How to **greet** people properly (Good morning, Hello, Hi)
- How to **thank** someone (Thank you, Thanks a lot)
- How to **ask for permission** (May I...? Can I...?)
- How to **introduce** yourself and others
- How to **apologize** (Sorry, Excuse me)
- Choosing the **right words** for different situations

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**What is Spoken and Written Expression?**
It means knowing the right thing to say in different situations. In real life, we speak differently to a teacher vs a friend!

**Common Expressions:**

| Situation | What to Say |
|-----------|-------------|
| Meeting someone in the morning | Good morning! |
| Meeting someone in the evening | Good evening! |
| Saying goodbye | Goodbye! / Bye! / See you! |
| Thanking someone | Thank you! / Thanks a lot! |
| Asking permission | May I...? / Can I...? |
| Saying sorry | Sorry / I'm sorry / Excuse me |
| Introducing yourself | Hello, my name is... / I am... |
| Asking someone's name | What is your name? |
| Offering help | Can I help you? / May I help you? |
| Wishing on birthday | Happy Birthday! |

**Formal vs Informal:**
- **Formal** (to teachers, elders): Good morning, Ma'am. May I come in?
- **Informal** (to friends): Hi! Can I come in?

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Expression
1. **Who** are you talking to? (teacher, friend, parent)
2. **What** is the situation? (greeting, asking, thanking)
3. **Be polite** — say please, thank you, sorry
