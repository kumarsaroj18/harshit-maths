## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- What **tense** means — it tells us when something happens
- **Simple Present**: things that happen regularly or are always true
- **Simple Past**: things that already happened (yesterday, last week)
- **Simple Future**: things that will happen later (tomorrow, next week)

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**What is Tense?**
Tense tells us **when** an action happens — now, before, or later.

**The Three Simple Tenses:**

| Tense | When | Example | Time Words |
|-------|------|---------|------------|
| Simple Present | Now, regularly, always | I **play** every day. | always, usually, every day, sometimes |
| Simple Past | Before, already done | I **played** yesterday. | yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020 |
| Simple Future | Later, not yet | I **will play** tomorrow. | tomorrow, next week, soon, later |

**Simple Present:**
- For habits: She **brushes** her teeth every morning.
- For facts: The sun **rises** in the east.
- Add **-s** or **-es** for he/she/it: He run**s**, She go**es**

**Simple Past:**
- Add **-ed** to most verbs: play → play**ed**, walk → walk**ed**
- Some verbs change completely: go → **went**, eat → **ate**, see → **saw**

**Simple Future:**
- Use **will + verb**: I **will** go, She **will** come
- Use **going to**: I am **going to** play

### 🧠 How to Choose the Right Tense
1. **Look for time words:** yesterday = past, tomorrow = future
2. **Ask:** Did it already happen? Is it happening now? Will it happen later?
3. **Match** the verb form to the time
