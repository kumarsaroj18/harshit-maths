# 🔍 Unit 2 — Odd One Out

**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 2 of 21**

> **Parent's Note:** For every question, ask him to say OUT LOUD why the other three belong together before picking the odd one. That habit is what wins Olympiad marks.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Finding the one item that does **not belong** with the rest
- Spotting the **hidden rule** that connects the other three (same group, same count, same shape, odd/even, multiple of a number)
- Looking carefully before deciding — sometimes the trick is in the **count**, not the picture!

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**The Big Idea:** Three (or more) things share something in common. One thing is different. That different one is the **Odd One Out**.

```
🍎  🍌  🍇  🚗
```
🍎, 🍌, 🍇 are all **fruits**. 🚗 is a **vehicle** — so 🚗 is the odd one out!

**Common hidden rules to look for:**
| Rule type | Example |
|---|---|
| Category | fruits vs. vehicle, animals vs. shape |
| Count | 3, 3, 3, 2 → the group of 2 is odd |
| Number property | even numbers vs. one odd number |
| Shape property | straight-line shapes vs. one curved shape |
| Multiple of a number | 5, 10, 15 (multiples of 5) vs. 16 |

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Say** what the first three items have in common, out loud.
2. **Check** the last item against that same rule.
3. **Pick** the one that breaks the rule.
