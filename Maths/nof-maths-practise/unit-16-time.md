## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Reading a clock: **o'clock** and **half past**
- Days of the week and months of the year, in order
- **AM** (morning) and **PM** (afternoon/evening/night)
- Simple elapsed time (how much time has passed)

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**Reading the clock:**
```
🕒 = 3 o'clock       (short hand on 3, long hand on 12)
🕜 = half past 1      (short hand between 1 and 2, long hand on 6)
```

**Handy facts to remember:**
| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Days in a week | 7 |
| Months in a year | 12 |
| Hours in a day | 24 |
| Minutes in an hour | 60 |
| Minutes in half an hour | 30 |

**AM and PM:** AM = morning (midnight to noon). PM = afternoon, evening, and night (noon to midnight).

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Look** at the short hand for the hour, the long hand for the minutes.
2. For "how many hours/days later," **count forward** one step at a time.
3. Remember the **order**: Sunday→Saturday for days, January→December for months.
