## 💡 Learn It First

**A thermometer** measures how hot or cold something is, in **degrees Celsius (°C)**.

```
🌡️
Higher number  =  Hotter
Lower number   =  Colder
```

**Everyday temperature facts:**
| Situation | Approximate Temperature |
|---|---|
| Ice / freezing water | 0°C (very cold) |
| A cool room | around 20°C |
| Healthy human body | around 37°C |
| A hot summer day | around 40°C |
| Boiling water | 100°C (very hot) |

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Compare the numbers** — the bigger number always means hotter.
2. **Think of real life** — ice is cold, boiling water is hot, your body is warm.
3. **Use words carefully**: "increase" means getting hotter, "decrease" means getting colder.
