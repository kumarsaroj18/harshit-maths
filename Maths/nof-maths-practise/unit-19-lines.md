**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 19 of 21**

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Straight lines vs. curved lines
- **Horizontal** (flat), **vertical** (up-down), and **slanting** (diagonal) lines
- **Open** figures (ends don't meet) vs. **closed** figures (ends meet)
- **Line of symmetry** — a line that divides a shape into two matching halves

---

## 💡 Learn It First

```
———      Straight line
〰️      Curved line

—        Horizontal line   (flat, left to right)
|        Vertical line     (straight up and down)
/        Slanting line     (diagonal)
```

**Open vs. Closed figures:**
```
▲  (closed — starts and ends at the same point)
⌣  (open — starts and ends at different points)
```

**Line of symmetry:** an imaginary line where, if you fold the shape along it, both halves match exactly.
```
   |
 ▲   →  fold down the middle, both sides match!
```

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Look** — is the line straight or does it curve?
2. **Check the direction** — flat (horizontal), up-down (vertical), or diagonal (slanting)?
3. For symmetry — **imagine folding** the shape in half. Do both sides match exactly?
