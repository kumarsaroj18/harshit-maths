# 🧊 Unit 20 — Shapes and Solids

**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 20 of 21**

> **Parent's Note:** Gather a dice, a ball, a can, a box, and an ice-cream cone (or a picture of one) — matching real objects to names is the fastest way to lock this in.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Naming solid (3D) shapes: **cube, cuboid, sphere, cylinder, cone**
- Counting **faces, edges, and corners** of solids
- Telling flat (2D) shapes apart from solid (3D) shapes
- Matching solids to real-world objects

---

## 💡 Learn It First

| Solid | Picture | Real object | Faces | Edges | Corners |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cube | 🎲 | Dice | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| Cuboid | 📦 | Box | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| Sphere | ⚽ | Ball | 1 (curved) | 0 | 0 |
| Cylinder | 🥫 | Can | 3 (2 flat + 1 curved) | 2 | 0 |
| Cone | 🍦 | Ice-cream cone | 2 (1 flat + 1 curved) | 1 | 1 |

**Key words:**
- **Face** = a flat (or curved) surface of the solid
- **Edge** = where two faces meet, forming a line
- **Corner (vertex)** = a point where edges meet

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Picture** the real object in your mind (a dice, a ball, a can).
2. **Count** faces first, then edges, then corners.
3. Remember: **round shapes** (sphere, cylinder, cone) have fewer or no corners.
