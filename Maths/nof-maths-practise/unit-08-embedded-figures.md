# 🔎 Unit 8 — Embedded Figures

**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 8 of 21**

> **Parent's Note:** Ask him to point at each shape with his finger while counting out loud — it stops him from counting the same shape twice or missing one.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Spotting simple shapes **hidden inside** a bigger picture
- Counting shapes carefully **without double-counting**
- Telling apart look-alikes — like a **square** (all sides equal) vs. a **rectangle** (sides unequal)

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**The Big Idea:** Every picture — a house, a rocket, a butterfly — is really just simple shapes put together!

```
   ▲          ← Triangle (roof)
  ▲▲▲
 ■■■■■        ← Square (body)
 ■■■■■
```
This house picture is made of **1 triangle + 1 square**.

**Watch out for tricky look-alikes:**
| Shape | Rule |
|---|---|
| Square | All 4 sides are EQUAL |
| Rectangle | Opposite sides equal, but longer/shorter |

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Point** at each part of the picture one at a time.
2. **Name** its shape (triangle, square, circle, rectangle...).
3. **Count** — keep a running tally so you don't count the same shape twice.
