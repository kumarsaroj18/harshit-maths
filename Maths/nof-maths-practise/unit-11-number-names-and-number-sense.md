# 💯 Unit 11 — Number Names & Number Sense (3-Digit Numbers)

**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 11 of 21**

> **Parent's Note:** Say numbers out loud while writing them ("Three hundred forty-five... 3, 4, 5") — hearing and writing together builds the number-name skill fastest.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Reading and writing **number names** for 3-digit numbers (e.g., 345 = Three Hundred Forty-Five)
- Finding numbers that come **before, after, and between**
- **Ascending** (small to big) and **Descending** (big to small) order
- Understanding **expanded form** (245 = 200 + 40 + 5)

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**Writing Number Names:**
```
345  →  Three Hundred Forty-Five
100  →  One Hundred
250  →  Two Hundred Fifty
```

**Expanded Form:** breaking a number into hundreds + tens + ones
```
345 = 300 + 40 + 5
```

**Before, After, Between:**
```
___ , 246 , ___     →   245 , 246 , 247
(before)     (after)
```

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Break** the number into hundreds, tens, and ones.
2. **Say** each part's name in order (hundreds first, then tens-and-ones together).
3. For before/after: **before** means subtract 1, **after** means add 1.
