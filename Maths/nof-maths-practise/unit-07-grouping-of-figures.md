# 🗂️ Unit 7 — Grouping of Figures

**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 7 of 21**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit is like sorting toys into two baskets. Ask him to name the RULE for each basket before deciding where a new item goes.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Sorting figures/numbers/animals into **two groups** based on a shared rule
- Deciding which group a **new item** belongs to
- Spotting when something belongs to **both** groups, or to **neither**

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**The Big Idea:** Look at the figures already sorted into Group 1 and Group 2, figure out the RULE for each group, then use that rule to sort a new figure.

```
Group 1: ■  ▬  ⬛     (all have 4 sides)
Group 2: ▲  ◺          (all have 3 sides)

New shape: ◆ (a diamond — it has 4 sides)
→ It goes into Group 1!
```

**Watch out for tricky ones:**
- A new item might fit **both** groups (like the number 10, which is a multiple of both 5 and 10).
- A new item might fit **neither** group (like a 3-digit number when the groups only have 1-digit and 2-digit numbers).

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Name** the rule for Group 1 out loud ("all shapes with 4 sides").
2. **Name** the rule for Group 2 out loud ("all shapes with 3 sides").
3. **Test** the new item against each rule before choosing.
