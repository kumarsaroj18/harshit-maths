## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Indoor vs Outdoor Games**

| Indoor Games 🏠 | Outdoor Games 🌳 |
|----------------|------------------|
| Chess | Cricket |
| Carrom | Football |
| Ludo | Hockey |
| Table Tennis | Kabaddi |

**2. Sports and Their Equipment**

| Sport | Equipment |
|-------|----------|
| Cricket 🏏 | Bat, ball, stumps |
| Football ⚽ | Football |
| Badminton 🏸 | Racket, shuttlecock |
| Tennis 🎾 | Racket, tennis ball |
| Hockey 🏑 | Stick, ball |

**3. Famous Indian Sports Players**

- **Sachin Tendulkar** — Cricket (God of Cricket)
- **Virat Kohli** — Cricket
- **P.V. Sindhu** — Badminton
- **Neeraj Chopra** — Javelin throw (Olympic gold)
- **Mary Kom** — Boxing

**4. Important Facts**

- **National sport of India**: Hockey
- **Most popular sport in India**: Cricket
- **Olympics**: Held every 4 years
