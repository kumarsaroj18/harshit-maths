---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Our Body Parts**

- **Head** — contains brain, eyes, ears, nose, mouth
- **Hands** — we use them to hold and write
- **Legs** — we use them to walk and run
- **Heart** — pumps blood in our body
- **Lungs** — help us breathe
- **Stomach** — digests the food we eat

**2. Five Sense Organs**

| Sense Organ | What It Does |
|-------------|-------------|
| Eyes 👀 | We see with our eyes |
| Ears 👂 | We hear with our ears |
| Nose 👃 | We smell with our nose |
| Tongue 👅 | We taste with our tongue |
| Skin 🖐️ | We feel (touch) with our skin |

**3. Healthy Habits**

- Brush teeth **twice a day**
- Take a **bath daily**
- Wash hands **before eating** and **after using toilet**
- Eat **fruits and vegetables**
- **Exercise** and play outdoor games
- Sleep **8-10 hours** every night
