## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know about **common machines** and gadgets

- Understand basic **science concepts**

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Common Machines & Gadgets**

| Machine | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| Television 📺 | Shows pictures and videos |
| Computer 💻 | Helps us work, play, and learn |
| Telephone 📞 | Helps us talk to people far away |
| Refrigerator 🧊 | Keeps food cold |
| Fan 🪭 | Gives us cool air |

**2. Sources of Light**

- **Sun** — biggest natural source of light
- **Electric bulb** — man-made light source
- **Candle** — gives light when burning
- **Torch/Flashlight** — portable light

**3. Sources of Energy**

- **Sun** — gives us solar energy
- **Wind** — gives us wind energy
- **Water** — gives us hydro energy
- **Food** — gives us energy to work

**4. Famous Scientists**

- **Thomas Edison** — invented the light bulb
- **Alexander Graham Bell** — invented the telephone
- **Wright Brothers** — invented the aeroplane

## ✅ Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|:---:|---|
| 1 | (C) Sun | The Sun is the biggest and main source of light on Earth |
| 2 | (D) Refrigerator | A refrigerator keeps food cold and fresh |
| 3 | (C) Telephone | We use a telephone or mobile phone to talk to people far away |
| 4 | (D) Fan | A fan or air conditioner gives us cool air |
| 5 | (C) Electric iron | We use an electric iron to remove wrinkles from clothes |
| 6 | (D) Television | Television (TV) shows us pictures, videos, and movies |
| 7 | (A) Sunlight | Plants need sunlight, water, and air to make food |
| 8 | (C) Sun | The Sun is a natural source of light; bulbs are man-made |
| 9 | (D) Energy | Food gives us energy to work, play, and grow |
| 10 | (B) Warmer | Ice melts and becomes water when heated or warmed |
| 11 | (A) Thomas Edison | Thomas Edison invented the practical electric light bulb |
| 12 | (B) It turns to steam | When water is heated, it turns into steam (water vapor) |
| 13 | (C) Alexander Graham Bell | Alexander Graham Bell invented the telephone |
| 14 | (C) Washing machine | A washing machine washes clothes automatically |
| 15 | (C) It attracts iron | A magnet can attract iron and steel objects |
| 16 | (B) TV remote | A TV remote runs on batteries |
| 17 | (B) Ice | When water freezes, it becomes ice |
| 18 | (A) Wright Brothers | The Wright Brothers (Orville and Wilbur) invented the aeroplane |
| 19 | (A) Vacuum cleaner | A vacuum cleaner sucks up dust and dirt from floors |
| 20 | (D) Sand | Glass is made from sand heated to very high temperatures |
| 21 | (A) Metal | Metals like copper and iron conduct electricity well |
| 22 | (B) Sunlight | Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity |
| 23 | (A) Microwave | A microwave oven heats food quickly |
| 24 | (D) CPU | CPU (Central Processing Unit) is the brain of a computer |
| 25 | (A) Wood | Wood is lighter than water, so it floats |
| 26 | (D) 100°C | Water boils at 100°C (degrees Celsius) |
| 27 | (A) 7 | Rainbow has 7 colours: Violet, Indigo, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red (VIBGYOR) |
| 28 | (B) 0°C | Water freezes to become ice at 0°C (degrees Celsius) |
| 29 | (B) Solid (like steel) | Sound travels fastest through solids, slower in liquids, and slowest in air |
| 30 | (B) Mars | Mars is called the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance |
