# 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Unit 1 — Me and My Family

**SOF IGKO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 1 of 11**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit covers family relationships, home, and basic personal information. Perfect for starting GK practice!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know your **family members** and their relationships
- Understand **roles** of different family members
- Learn about **home** and things we use daily
- Know important **personal information** (address, phone)
- Understand **good habits** at home

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Family Members**

- **Father** (Papa/Dad) and **Mother** (Mummy/Mom) are your parents
- Your **parents' parents** are your grandparents (Dada-Dadi or Nana-Nani)
- Your **father's brother** is your uncle (Chacha/Tau)
- Your **mother's brother** is also your uncle (Mama)
- Your **uncle's or aunt's children** are your cousins

**2. Our Home**

- Kitchen is where we **cook food**
- Bedroom is where we **sleep**
- Bathroom is where we **take bath** and brush teeth
- Living room is where **family sits together**

**3. Important Information**

- Everyone should know their **full name**
- Know your **home address** and **phone number**
- Know your **parents' names**
