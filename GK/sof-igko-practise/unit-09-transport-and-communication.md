# 🚗 Unit 9 — Transport and Communication

**SOF IGKO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 9 of 11**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit covers different modes of transport and ways we communicate. Important for everyday awareness!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know about **land, water, and air transport**
- Learn about **different vehicles** and what they do
- Understand **means of communication**
- Know about **traffic rules** and road safety
- Learn about **modern communication** — phones, internet

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Types of Transport**

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Land 🚗 | Car, Bus, Train, Bicycle, Motorcycle |
| Water 🚢 | Ship, Boat, Ferry |
| Air ✈️ | Aeroplane, Helicopter |

**2. Means of Communication**

| Old Ways | Modern Ways |
|----------|-------------|
| Letters ✉️ | Email 📧 |
| Drums | Mobile phone 📱 |
| Pigeons | Video calls 💻 |
| Messengers | Internet 🌐 |

**3. Traffic Rules**

- **Red light**: Stop 🔴
- **Yellow light**: Get ready 🟡
- **Green light**: Go 🟢
- Always walk on the **footpath/sidewalk**
- Use **zebra crossing** to cross the road
- Look **left and right** before crossing

**4. Emergency Numbers**

- Police: **100**
- Fire: **101**
- Ambulance: **102** or **108**
