## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know different **types of animals** — wild, pet, farm animals
- Learn where animals **live** (their homes)
- Understand what different animals **eat**
- Know **parts of a plant** and their uses
- Learn about **baby animals** and their names

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Types of Animals**

- **Pet animals**: Dog, Cat, Parrot, Fish, Rabbit — we keep them at home
- **Farm animals**: Cow, Buffalo, Hen, Goat, Sheep — they give us food or help us
- **Wild animals**: Lion, Tiger, Elephant, Bear, Wolf — they live in forests

**2. Animal Homes**

- Dog lives in a **kennel**
- Bird lives in a **nest**
- Bee lives in a **hive**
- Lion lives in a **den**
- Rabbit lives in a **burrow**
- Fish lives in **water**

**3. What Animals Eat**

- **Herbivores** eat only plants: Cow, Deer, Elephant, Rabbit
- **Carnivores** eat only meat: Lion, Tiger, Wolf
- **Omnivores** eat both: Bear, Crow, Pig, Humans

**4. Parts of a Plant**

- **Root** — under the ground, takes in water
- **Stem** — holds the plant up, carries water
- **Leaf** — makes food for the plant
- **Flower** — becomes fruit, has seeds
- **Fruit** — has seeds inside
