# 🌍 Unit 5 — The World Around Us

**SOF IGKO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 5 of 11**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit covers community helpers, places in our neighborhood, and basic world knowledge. Great for social awareness!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know about **community helpers** and their work
- Learn about **places** in our neighbourhood
- Understand **different countries** and continents
- Know about **directions** — North, South, East, West
- Learn about **world landmarks** and facts

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Community Helpers**

| Helper | What They Do |
|--------|-------------|
| Doctor 👨‍⚕️ | Treats sick people |
| Teacher 👩‍🏫 | Teaches students |
| Police 👮 | Keeps us safe |
| Firefighter 🧑‍🚒 | Puts out fires |
| Postman 📬 | Delivers letters |
| Farmer 👨‍🌾 | Grows food |

**2. Places in Our Neighbourhood**

- **School** — where we study
- **Hospital** — where sick people get treatment
- **Post Office** — where we send letters
- **Market** — where we buy vegetables and groceries
- **Bank** — where we keep money safe

**3. Continents of the World**

There are 7 continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Europe, Australia, Antarctica

**4. Directions**

- The Sun rises in the **East**
- The Sun sets in the **West**
- **North** is up on the map
- **South** is down on the map
