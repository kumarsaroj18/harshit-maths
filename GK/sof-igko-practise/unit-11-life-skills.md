## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know about **good manners** and polite behaviour
- Learn **safety rules** at home and outside

- Know about **honesty and kindness**
- Learn about **being responsible**

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Good Manners (Magic Words)**

| When | Say |
|------|-----|
| Asking for something | Please 🙏 |
| Receiving something | Thank you 😊 |
| Making a mistake | Sorry 😔 |
| Meeting someone | Hello/Namaste 🙏 |
| Leaving | Goodbye/Bye |

**2. Safety Do's and Don'ts**

✅ **Do's:**
- Look left and right before crossing the road
- Tell parents where you are going
- Wash hands before eating
- Wear helmet while cycling
- Say no to strangers offering food or rides

❌ **Don'ts:**
- Don't play with fire or sharp objects
- Don't touch electrical switches with wet hands
- Don't take medicines without parents' permission
- Don't share personal information with strangers
- Don't bully others

**3. Being a Good Person**

- Always tell the **truth**
- **Share** with others
- Be **kind** to everyone
- **Help** those in need
- **Respect** elders and teachers
