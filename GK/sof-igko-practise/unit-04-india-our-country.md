## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know our **national symbols** — flag, anthem, bird, animal
- Learn about **important festivals** of India
- Know **famous monuments** and places

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. National Symbols of India**

| Symbol | Name |
|--------|------|
| National Flag 🇮🇳 | Tricolour (Tiranga) — Saffron, White, Green with Ashoka Chakra |
| National Anthem 🎵 | Jana Gana Mana |
| National Song 🎶 | Vande Mataram |
| National Bird 🦚 | Peacock |
| National Animal 🐅 | Tiger |
| National Flower 🌸 | Lotus |
| National Fruit 🥭 | Mango |

**2. Important Facts**

- Capital of India: **New Delhi**
- India's Independence Day: **15th August** (1947)
- Republic Day: **26th January** (1950)
- Gandhi Jayanti: **2nd October** (Mahatma Gandhi's birthday)

**3. Famous Monuments**

- **Taj Mahal** — Agra (built by Shah Jahan)
- **Red Fort** — Delhi
- **Qutub Minar** — Delhi
- **Gateway of India** — Mumbai
