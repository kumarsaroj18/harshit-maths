# 🌏 Unit 7 — Earth and Environment

**SOF IGKO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 7 of 11**

> **Parent's Note:** This unit covers our planet, weather, seasons, and environmental awareness. Important for understanding our world!

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Know about our planet **Earth** and its features
- Learn about **weather** and **seasons**
- Understand **day and night** and why they happen
- Know about **water bodies** — rivers, oceans, lakes
- Learn about **keeping our environment clean**

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**1. Our Planet Earth**

- Earth is the **third planet** from the Sun
- Earth is also called the **Blue Planet** (because of water)
- Earth has **one moon**
- Earth **rotates** (spins) causing day and night
- Earth **revolves** around the Sun causing seasons

**2. Four Seasons**

| Season | Weather |
|--------|--------|
| Summer ☀️ | Very hot |
| Monsoon/Rainy 🌧️ | Lots of rain |
| Autumn 🍂 | Leaves fall |
| Winter ❄️ | Very cold |

**3. Water Bodies**

- **River** — flowing fresh water
- **Lake** — still fresh water surrounded by land
- **Ocean/Sea** — large salt water body
- **Pond** — small water body

**4. Keep Environment Clean**

- Don't throw garbage on roads
- Plant trees
- Save water
- Reduce, Reuse, Recycle
