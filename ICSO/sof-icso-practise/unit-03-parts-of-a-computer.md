## 🎯 What You You Will Learn

- Naming the main parts of a computer: keyboard, mouse, monitor, CPU, printer, speaker
- The difference between **input devices** (we give the computer something) and **output devices** (the computer gives us something)
- What the **CPU** does

---

## 💡 Learn It First

```
🖥️ Monitor       — shows pictures and words (OUTPUT)
⌨️ Keyboard       — types letters and numbers (INPUT)
🖱️ Mouse          — points and clicks (INPUT)
🖨️ Printer        — prints your work on paper (OUTPUT)
🔊 Speaker        — plays sound (OUTPUT)
🎤 Microphone     — records your voice (INPUT)
```

**INPUT devices** — we use these to GIVE something TO the computer (words, clicks, sound, pictures).
**OUTPUT devices** — these GIVE something back to US (pictures, words, sound, printed paper).

```
INPUT  →  COMPUTER (CPU thinks/processes)  →  OUTPUT
```

The **CPU** (Central Processing Unit) is the **brain** of the computer — it does all the thinking and processing, hidden inside the computer's box.

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Ask:** does this part let ME put something INTO the computer? → it's INPUT.
2. **Ask:** does this part give something back TO me (to see/hear/hold)? → it's OUTPUT.
3. **Remember:** the CPU is the "brain" — it's neither input nor output, it just thinks.
