# 🧠 Unit 1 — Logical Reasoning

**SOF ICSO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 1 of 7**

> **Parent's Note:** This section is worth only 5 marks in the real ICSO paper (out of 35 questions), but it warms up the same thinking skills used everywhere else in the exam. If your child has already done the Maths Olympiad reasoning units, this will feel familiar — that's intentional!

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Spotting **patterns** and what comes next
- Finding the **odd one out** in a group
- Comparing sizes: **measuring units**
- Naming **shapes** and counting sides/corners
- Completing **analogies** (A is to B as C is to ___)
- **Ranking** people/things in order
- **Grouping** figures by a shared rule
- Cracking simple **codes**
- Spotting shapes **hidden** inside a picture

---

## 💡 Learn It First

This unit mixes ALL the reasoning skills tested in Section 1 of the real ICSO exam, a little bit of each:

| Skill | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Pattern | What small group of things keeps repeating? |
| Odd One Out | What do 3 of them share that the 4th doesn't? |
| Analogy | What is the connection between the first pair? |
| Ranking | Who/what comes first, tallest, heaviest? |
| Coding | What rule turns one letter/number into another? |

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Read** the question twice before looking at the options.
2. **Say the rule out loud** in your own words.
3. **Test** your rule against the options before picking one.

## ✅ Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|:---:|---|
| 1 | (B) 🟢 | Blue, Green repeats — after Blue (7th) comes Green |
| 2 | (A) 🚌 | The rest are fruits; a bus is a vehicle |
| 3 | (B) School bus | A bus is much bigger and longer than a pencil |
| 4 | (C) 4 | A square always has 4 sides |
| 5 | (D) Hive | A bird lives in a nest; a bee lives in a hive |
| 6 | (A) Tom | Tom > Sam > Rai, so Tom is the tallest |
| 7 | (B) ● | A circle is the only round shape here |
| 8 | (B) EPH | D→E, O→P, G→H (each letter moves 1 forward) |
| 9 | (D) Triangle | The pointed top part of the house is a triangle |
| 10 | (B) 10 | Add 2 each time: 8 + 2 = 10 |
| 11 | (A) ▲ | ▲, ● repeats — the 5th spot lines up with ▲ |
| 12 | (D) 7 | 2, 4, 6 are even numbers; 7 is odd |
| 13 | (B) Kilograms | Weight is measured in kilograms |
| 14 | (A) 5 | A pentagon has 5 corners |
| 15 | (D) Puppy | A dog's baby is called a puppy |
| 16 | (A) 4th | Position from right = 6 − 3 + 1 = 4th |
| 17 | (B) Group 1 | A diamond has 4 sides, matching Group 1's rule |
| 18 | (A) 5 | C = 3, B = 2, so 3 + 2 = 5 |
| 19 | (D) 4 | 1 square + 1 rectangle + 2 circles = 4 shapes |
| 20 | (C) 25 | Add 5 each time: 20 + 5 = 25 |
| 21 | (B) 🐔 | The rest have 4 legs; a hen has 2 legs |
| 22 | (B) Bucket | A bucket is much bigger than a cup |
| 23 | (C) Circle | A circle is round and smooth, with no corners |
| 24 | (D) Cutting | Scissors are used for cutting |
| 25 | (A) C | A > B > C in weight, so C is the lightest |
| 26 | (C) 15 | The jump grows each time: +2, +3, +4, then +5 → 10 + 5 = 15 |
| 27 | (A) GOD | Reading "DOG" backwards gives "GOD" |
| 28 | (D) 6 | Total = 2 (from left) + 5 (from right) − 1 = 6 |

| 30 | (C) Group 1 | 10 is an even number |
