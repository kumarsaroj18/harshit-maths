## 💡 Learn It First

**MS Paint** is a program used to draw and colour pictures on a computer.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| ✏️ Pencil | Draws thin, freehand lines |
| 🖌️ Brush | Draws thicker, textured strokes |
| 🧽 Eraser | Removes part of a drawing |
| 🪣 Fill with colour | Fills a whole closed area with one colour |
| ⬛ Shapes | Draws neat shapes — rectangle, circle, line |
| 🔤 Text | Types words onto the picture |
| 🎨 Colour palette | Where you pick your drawing colour |

**Fixing mistakes:** Click **Undo** to take back your last action. Always **Save** your picture when you're done, so you can open it again later.

**Watch out:** The Fill tool colours a WHOLE closed area. If the outline has a gap, the colour can "leak" out and colour more than you wanted!

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Picture** what you want to do — draw a thin line? Colour a big area? Fix a mistake?
2. **Match** it to the ONE tool built for exactly that job.
3. **Remember**: a closed shape with no gaps is needed for Fill to work properly.
