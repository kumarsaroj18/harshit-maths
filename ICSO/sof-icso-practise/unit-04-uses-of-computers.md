# 🏥 Unit 4 — Uses of Computers

**SOF ICSO Practice | Class 2 | Unit 4 of 7**

> **Parent's Note:** Next time you're at a shop, bank, or clinic together, point out where you spot a computer in use — it turns this whole unit into a fun "I Spy" game.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Places where computers help people do their work: **school, hospital, bank, home, airport, shop**
- How computers help with **learning, communication, and entertainment**
- Why computers make many jobs **faster and easier**

---

## 💡 Learn It First

Computers help people in almost every place:

| Place | How a computer helps |
|---|---|
| 🏫 School | Teaching, learning games, storing marks |
| 🏥 Hospital | Storing patient records |
| 🏦 Bank | Keeping track of people's money safely |
| 🏠 Home | Homework, games, videos |
| ✈️ Airport | Booking tickets, tracking flights |
| 🏪 Shop | Billing, keeping track of stock |

**Computers are useful because they help us:**
- **Learn** — educational videos and games
- **Communicate** — emails, messages, video calls
- **Work faster** — calculating, storing, and finding information quickly
- **Have fun** — games, movies, music

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Picture the place** in the question — a hospital, school, bank, shop.
2. **Ask:** what real work happens there that a computer could help with?
3. **Rule out** answers that have nothing to do with computers (cooking, growing plants, sleeping).
