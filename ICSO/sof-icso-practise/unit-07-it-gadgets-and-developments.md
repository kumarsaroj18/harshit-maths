## 💡 Learn It First

**IT gadgets** are electronic devices that help us with everyday tasks:
```
📱 Smartphone   ⌚ Smartwatch   📷 Digital camera   💻 Tablet
```

**An app** is a small program that does ONE job well — a game app, a calculator app, a drawing app.

**The Internet** lets computers and devices talk to each other and share information — like video calling someone far away, or looking up information.

**Computerization** means everyday objects now have small computers inside them — like a "smart" TV or a digital watch — making them do more than they used to.

**A robot** is a machine that follows instructions (a program) to do tasks — it does not think or decide on its own, just like a computer.

**Staying safe online:** Always use the internet **with a parent or elder nearby**, and never share your name, address, school, or password with strangers online.

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Ask:** is this a real gadget/app, or something unrelated (like food or an animal)?
2. **Remember:** apps, robots, and smart gadgets ALL follow instructions — none of them think on their own.
3. **Think safety first** — when a question is about the internet, the safest, adult-supervised answer is usually correct.
