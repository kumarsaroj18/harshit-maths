## 💡 Learn It First

**A computer is an electronic machine** that follows instructions to store information, do calculations, and show us pictures, words, and videos.

```
YOU give an instruction  →  COMPUTER follows it  →  COMPUTER shows the result
```

**The most important rule:** A computer has **no brain or feelings of its own**. It cannot think, decide, or do anything unless a person tells it what to do. It is smart because it follows instructions super fast — not because it thinks by itself.

**Turning a computer ON and OFF the right way:**
| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| Turning ON | Press the **Power button** |
| Turning OFF | **Save your work**, close programs, then use **Shut Down** — never just pull the plug! |

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Ask:** does this need a person to give an instruction first? (The answer is almost always YES.)
2. **Remember:** a computer is a machine, not a living, thinking being.
3. **Think** about the SAFE way to do something (like shutting down) before picking an answer.
