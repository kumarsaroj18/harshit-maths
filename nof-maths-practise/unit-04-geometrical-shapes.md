## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Naming flat (2D) shapes: circle, triangle, square, rectangle, pentagon, hexagon
- Naming solid (3D) shapes: cube, sphere, cylinder, cone, cuboid
- Counting **sides** and **corners**
- Matching shapes to everyday objects

---

## 💡 Learn It First

**Flat (2D) shapes** — you can draw them on paper. They have sides and corners but no thickness.

```
●        ▲          ■         ▬          ⬠           ⬡
Circle  Triangle   Square   Rectangle  Pentagon    Hexagon
0 sides  3 sides   4 sides   4 sides    5 sides     6 sides
0 corners 3 corners 4 corners 4 corners 5 corners   6 corners
```

**Solid (3D) shapes** — you can hold them, they take up space.

```
🎲          ⚽        🥫           📦        🍦
Cube      Sphere   Cylinder     Cuboid     Cone
```

**Golden Rule:** For any straight-sided flat shape, the **number of sides = number of corners**.

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Look** at the outline — is it flat (2D) or does it take up space (3D)?
2. **Count** the sides carefully, one at a time, going around.
3. **Match** — the number of corners will always equal the number of straight sides.
