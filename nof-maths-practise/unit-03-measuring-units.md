## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Which **tool** measures what (length, weight, capacity, time, temperature)
- Comparing objects: **longer/shorter, heavier/lighter, more/less**
- The right **unit** to use for small vs. big things (cm vs. m, g vs. kg)
- Simple measuring word problems

---

## 💡 Learn It First

| We want to measure... | We use... | Units |
|---|---|---|
| **Length** (how long/tall) | 📏 Ruler / measuring tape | centimetres (cm), metres (m) |
| **Weight** (how heavy) | ⚖️ Weighing scale | grams (g), kilograms (kg) |
| **Capacity** (how much liquid) | 🥤 Measuring cup/jug | millilitres (mL), litres (L) |
| **Time** | 🕐 Clock | minutes, hours |
| **Temperature** (how hot/cold) | 🌡️ Thermometer | degrees |

**Small thing → small unit.** A button is weighed in **grams**; an elephant is weighed in **kilograms**. A pencil is measured in **centimetres**; a playground is measured in **metres**.

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Decide** — am I measuring length, weight, capacity, time, or temperature?
2. **Pick** the correct tool or unit for the SIZE of the thing.
3. **Compare** carefully — check which side of the balance goes down (that side is heavier!).
