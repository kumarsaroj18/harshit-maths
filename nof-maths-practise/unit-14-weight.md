# ⚖️ Unit 14 — Weight

**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 14 of 21**

> **Parent's Note:** A kitchen weighing scale is perfect here — weigh a few household items together with him before starting.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Units of weight: **gram (g)** and **kilogram (kg)**
- The conversion rule: **1 kg = 1000 g**
- Adding and subtracting weights
- Comparing and ordering weights

---

## 💡 Learn It First

```
1 kilogram (kg)  =  1000 grams (g)
```

**Converting kilograms to grams:** multiply by 1000
```
2 kg = 2 × 1000 = 2000 g
```

**Converting grams to kilograms:** divide by 1000 (works evenly for multiples of 1000)
```
3000 g = 3000 ÷ 1000 = 3 kg
```

**Adding/subtracting weights** — keep the unit the same:
```
250 g + 300 g = 550 g
1 kg 200 g  =  1200 g   (1 kg = 1000 g, so 1000 + 200 = 1200)
```

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Check the units** — are both weights in g, or in kg? Make them match first.
2. **Convert** using 1 kg = 1000 g if needed.
3. **Add, subtract, or compare** the numbers normally.
