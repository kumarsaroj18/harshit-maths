## 💡 Learn It First

**Indian Money:**
```
Coins:  ₹1   ₹2   ₹5   ₹10
Notes:  ₹10   ₹20   ₹50   ₹100   ₹200   ₹500
```

**Finding the total:** just add up all the amounts.
```
₹10 + ₹20 + ₹5 = ₹35
```

**Finding change:** subtract the cost from the amount paid.
```
You pay ₹50 for something that costs ₹35.
Change = ₹50 − ₹35 = ₹15
```

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Add up** all the coins/notes to find the total money.
2. To buy something, **subtract** its price from your total money.
3. For change, **subtract** the price from the amount you handed over.
