# 📐 Unit 13 — Length

**NOF Maths Olympiad Practice | Class 2 | Unit 13 of 21**

> **Parent's Note:** A kitchen measuring tape marked in cm and m is the best prop for this unit — let him measure real things at home.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

- Units of length: **centimetre (cm)** and **metre (m)**
- The conversion rule: **1 m = 100 cm**
- Adding and subtracting lengths
- Comparing and ordering lengths

---

## 💡 Learn It First

```
1 metre (m)  =  100 centimetres (cm)
```

**Converting metres to centimetres:** multiply by 100
```
3 m = 3 × 100 = 300 cm
```

**Converting centimetres to metres:** divide by 100 (only works evenly for multiples of 100)
```
500 cm = 500 ÷ 100 = 5 m
```

**Adding/subtracting lengths** — just like regular numbers, but keep the unit the same:
```
25 cm + 30 cm = 55 cm
1 m 20 cm  =  120 cm   (1 m = 100 cm, so 100 + 20 = 120)
```

### 🧠 How to Solve — 3 Easy Steps
1. **Check the units** — are both lengths in cm, or in m? Make them match first.
2. **Convert** using 1 m = 100 cm if needed.
3. **Add, subtract, or compare** the numbers normally.
